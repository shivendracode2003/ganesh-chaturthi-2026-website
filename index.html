Days of the festival

Most families and mandals send Bappa off after 1.5, 3, 5, 7, 9 or 11 days.

  1. 14 SepSthapana and pran pratishtha on Chaturthi
  2. 15 SepGauri avahan, the 1.5-day visarjan
  3. 16 to 18 SepVisarjan for the 3 and 5-day homes
  4. 20 to 22 SepVisarjan for the 7 and 9-day homes
  5. 25 SepAnant Chaturdashi, the grand procession
वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ ।
निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा ॥

O Lord of the curved trunk and mighty form, bright as a million suns: remove every obstacle from all that I begin.
